## Front matter

Every post starts with a title, a one or two sentence description (it becomes the card summary and the search snippet), a date, and optional tags. A `cover` image, placed next to the file, replaces the generated complex on the card.

<Note>The card for each post draws a small complex seeded from the file name, so every post gets its own emblem without any image work.</Note>

## Prose and sidenotes

Body text sits in a reading column of about 68 characters. A sidenote sits in the margin on wide screens and under the paragraph on narrow ones.<Sidenote n={1}>Like this one.</Sidenote> Links are teal and always underlined, as in [the Polytope docs](https://dario-loi.github.io/polytope/).
